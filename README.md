### 🔧 Projects I can share

#### 🔍 INSPECTRA: finding defects on a production line in real time
A two-stage vision pipeline. YOLOv8 finds where a defect is, and EfficientNet decides what kind it is. I exported both models with ONNX and TensorRT to reach **30-60 FPS**, and wired the results into InfluxDB and Grafana for live monitoring, with GPT-4o summarising anomalies.

EfficientNet classification reached **93.5% accuracy**. This work was published at **ICCCSS 2025 (SCITEPRESS, Scopus indexed)**.

`Python` `PyTorch` `YOLOv8` `EfficientNet` `OpenCV` `ONNX` `TensorRT` `Flask` `InfluxDB` `Grafana`
